# Legal Review Process

## Overview

All educational content in the Growth Training app must undergo legal review to ensure compliance with federal and state regulations, particularly FDA and FTC requirements for health-related content and advertising. This document outlines the legal review process, reviewer qualifications, and regulatory compliance requirements.

## Purpose

The legal review process serves to:
- Ensure compliance with FDA regulations on health claims
- Verify FTC compliance for advertising and consumer protection
- Identify prohibited or misleading health claims
- Assess liability risks and legal exposure
- Prevent false advertising or deceptive marketing
- Protect company from regulatory enforcement actions
- Document due diligence for regulatory defense

## Legal Reviewer Qualifications

### Required Qualifications

All legal reviewers must meet the following minimum requirements:

1. **Active Bar Membership**: Current, active member of state bar in good standing
2. **Relevant Experience**: Minimum 5 years experience in healthcare law, FDA/FTC compliance, or advertising law
3. **Regulatory Knowledge**: Demonstrated expertise in FDA and FTC regulations
4. **No Conflicts of Interest**: No conflicts that could compromise objective review
5. **Professional Liability Insurance**: Current errors & omissions insurance

### Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with digital health products or mobile health apps
- FDA regulatory affairs certification (RAC)
- Prior experience with FTC enforcement actions
- Knowledge of Section 230 and digital platform liability
- Experience with HIPAA compliance and health data privacy
- Familiarity with state-specific health advertising laws

### Reviewer Vetting Process

Before engaging a legal reviewer:

1. **Credential Verification**
   - Verify active bar membership with state bar association
   - Check for disciplinary actions or ethics violations
   - Confirm areas of practice and expertise
   - Verify professional liability insurance coverage

2. **Conflict of Interest Screening**
   - Complete conflict of interest questionnaire
   - Disclose representation of competitors or adverse parties
   - Disclose financial interests in related industries
   - Sign conflict of interest agreement

3. **Engagement Agreement**
   - Execute legal services agreement or engagement letter
   - Define scope of legal review responsibilities
   - Establish fee structure and billing terms
   - Clarify attorney-client privilege and confidentiality
   - Address indemnification and liability limits

## Legal Review Checklist

For each article under review, the legal reviewer must evaluate:

### 1. FDA Compliance

- [ ] **Device Claims**: No claims that product is a medical device subject to FDA regulation
- [ ] **Drug Claims**: No claims that product treats, cures, mitigates, or prevents disease
- [ ] **Structure/Function Claims**: Structure/function claims are permissible and properly worded
- [ ] **Disease Claims**: No express or implied disease claims without FDA approval
- [ ] **Clinical Studies**: Clinical study references comply with FDA guidance
- [ ] **Labeling Compliance**: Content doesn't constitute "labeling" requiring FDA review

**FDA Issues Identified**: _Document any FDA compliance concerns_

### 2. FTC Compliance

- [ ] **Truth in Advertising**: All claims are truthful and not misleading
- [ ] **Substantiation**: Health/performance claims are adequately substantiated
- [ ] **Clear Disclosures**: Material limitations and conditions are clearly disclosed
- [ ] **Endorsements**: Any testimonials comply with FTC endorsement guides
- [ ] **Results Claims**: No unsubstantiated claims of typical results
- [ ] **Before/After Images**: If used, comply with FTC guidance on substantiation
- [ ] **Deceptive Practices**: No deceptive, unfair, or abusive practices

**FTC Issues Identified**: _Document any FTC compliance concerns_

### 3. Prohibited Health Claims

- [ ] **No Cure Claims**: Content doesn't claim to cure any condition
- [ ] **No Treatment Claims**: Doesn't claim to treat medical conditions
- [ ] **No Prevention Claims**: Doesn't claim to prevent disease or medical conditions
- [ ] **No Diagnostic Claims**: Doesn't claim to diagnose medical conditions
- [ ] **No Efficacy Guarantees**: Doesn't guarantee specific health outcomes
- [ ] **No Medical Advice**: Clearly states content is educational, not medical advice

**Prohibited Claims Found**: _List any prohibited claims requiring revision_

### 4. Permissible Claims Assessment

- [ ] **General Wellness**: Claims limited to general wellness (if applicable)
- [ ] **Structure/Function**: Structure/function claims properly qualified
- [ ] **Educational Content**: Content is clearly educational/informational
- [ ] **Exercise Claims**: Exercise/training claims appropriately substantiated
- [ ] **Lifestyle Claims**: Lifestyle and wellness claims are reasonable
- [ ] **User Experience**: User experiences presented as individual, not typical

**Permissible Claims Analysis**: _Assess which claims are legally defensible_

### 5. Disclaimer Adequacy

- [ ] **Medical Consultation Advisory**: Advises consulting healthcare provider
- [ ] **No Guarantee Disclaimer**: Disclaims guarantees of specific results
- [ ] **Individual Variation**: Acknowledges individual results vary
- [ ] **Risk Disclosure**: Discloses inherent risks of physical activity
- [ ] **Age Restriction**: Restricts to adults 18+
- [ ] **Not Medical Advice**: Clearly disclaims providing medical advice
- [ ] **Disclaimer Prominence**: Disclaimers are sufficiently prominent and clear

**Disclaimer Assessment**: _Evaluate legal sufficiency of disclaimers_

### 6. Liability Risk Assessment

- [ ] **Malpractice Risk**: Low risk of medical malpractice claims
- [ ] **Product Liability**: Low risk of product liability claims
- [ ] **Negligence Claims**: Content doesn't create unreasonable risk of negligence claims
- [ ] **False Advertising**: Low risk of false advertising claims or enforcement
- [ ] **Consumer Protection**: Complies with state consumer protection laws
- [ ] **Class Action Risk**: Low risk of consumer class action litigation

**Liability Concerns**: _Identify potential legal exposure and risk level_

### 7. Intellectual Property

- [ ] **Copyright Compliance**: No copyright infringement issues
- [ ] **Fair Use**: Citations and excerpts qualify as fair use
- [ ] **Trademark**: No unauthorized trademark use
- [ ] **Publicity Rights**: No unauthorized use of names/likenesses
- [ ] **Plagiarism**: Content is original or properly attributed

**IP Issues**: _Note any intellectual property concerns_

### 8. Privacy and Data

- [ ] **Privacy Policy**: Content collection/use complies with privacy policy
- [ ] **Data Minimization**: Doesn't encourage collection of unnecessary personal data
- [ ] **Health Data**: Complies with health data privacy requirements
- [ ] **Children's Privacy**: Complies with COPPA (if applicable to under-13 users)
- [ ] **International**: Complies with GDPR and other international privacy laws (if applicable)

**Privacy Concerns**: _Document privacy and data issues_

## Legal Review Process Workflow

### Step 1: Pre-Review Preparation

Before submitting for legal review:
1. Medical review completed and approved
2. Content team has implemented medical reviewer's corrections
3. All citations verified and accessible
4. Marketing/promotional context provided (if applicable)
5. Competitive content examples provided for context

### Step 2: Legal Review Assignment

1. Assign appropriate legal reviewer based on regulatory expertise
2. Provide reviewer with:
   - Article content
   - Medical review report
   - Citation list
   - Legal review checklist
   - Regulatory guidance documents
   - Competitive content samples
3. Establish review timeline (typically 5-10 business days)

### Step 3: Legal Review Conduct

Reviewer:
1. Reviews content for regulatory compliance
2. Identifies prohibited claims or misleading statements
3. Assesses disclaimer adequacy
4. Evaluates liability risks
5. Completes legal review checklist
6. Prepares detailed review report with recommendations
7. Provides mark-up of content with suggested revisions

### Step 4: Review Response

Upon receiving legal review report:

**If APPROVED**:
1. File privileged review report in `docs/operations/compliance/legal-reviews/[article-id]-legal-review.md`
2. Update compliance checklist
3. Proceed to risk assessment

**If APPROVED WITH REVISIONS**:
1. Content team implements required legal revisions
2. Legal counsel reviews revised version
3. Obtain final legal approval
4. File review report
5. Proceed to risk assessment

**If REJECTED**:
1. Assess whether content can be salvaged with major revision
2. May require complete rewrite to achieve compliance
3. If compliance unachievable, content is archived
4. Document reasons for rejection for future reference

### Step 5: Documentation

All legal reviews must be properly documented:
- Review report saved as `[article-id]-legal-review.md`
- Stored in privileged `docs/operations/compliance/legal-reviews/` directory
- Compliance checklist updated
- Review added to compliance audit log
- **Note**: Legal review reports are attorney-client privileged

## Escalation Procedures

If legal issues arise requiring escalation:

### Minor Issues
- Reviewer provides revision recommendations
- Content team implements revisions
- Reviewer verifies compliance

### Moderate Issues
- Legal reviewer consults with General Counsel
- Risk assessment updated
- May require additional insurance review
- Business decision on acceptable risk level

### Major Issues
- Immediate escalation to General Counsel
- Senior management briefed on risks
- May require outside regulatory counsel
- Publication halted pending resolution
- Business risk/benefit analysis conducted

### Critical Issues
- CEO/Board notification
- Outside specialized counsel engaged
- Potential voluntary FDA/FTC consultation
- Content likely rejected for publication
- Documentation preserved for regulatory defense

## Periodic Re-Review Requirements

Legal content requires periodic re-review due to evolving regulations:

### Re-Review Triggers

- **Annual Review**: All content reviewed annually for regulatory changes
- **Regulatory Changes**: FDA or FTC issues new guidance affecting content
- **Enforcement Actions**: FDA/FTC takes enforcement action in related area
- **Case Law**: Relevant court decisions change legal landscape
- **Complaint Triggers**: User complaints or regulatory inquiries
- **Business Changes**: Company structure or product offerings change

### Re-Review Process

1. Flag content for re-review based on trigger
2. Assign to original legal counsel or qualified substitute
3. Conduct focused review on:
   - New regulatory guidance
   - Recent enforcement actions
   - Changes in legal interpretation
   - Evolving industry standards
4. Update content if needed to maintain compliance
5. Document re-review and any changes
6. Reset review schedule

## Confidentiality and Privilege

All legal review materials are confidential and privileged:

- **Attorney-Client Privilege**: Legal reviews are privileged communications
- **Work Product Doctrine**: Draft revisions and analysis are protected work product
- **Confidentiality Requirement**: Strict confidentiality of all review materials
- **Privilege Log**: Maintain privilege log for legal review documents
- **Waiver Risk**: Do not disclose legal reviews to third parties (waives privilege)

## Quality Assurance

To ensure quality of legal reviews:

- Annual audit of compliance with regulatory requirements
- Monitoring of FDA/FTC enforcement trends
- Legal reviewer performance evaluation based on:
  - Accuracy of regulatory analysis
  - Quality of revision recommendations
  - Timeliness of reviews
  - Risk prediction accuracy
- Continuing legal education requirements for reviewers
- Annual regulatory update training for legal team

## Litigation Hold

If litigation or investigation occurs:

1. **Immediate Hold**: Suspend any document destruction
2. **Preserve All Materials**: Retain all versions of content and reviews
3. **Legal Counsel Direction**: Outside litigation counsel directs preservation
4. **Employee Notice**: All relevant employees notified of hold
5. **IT Preservation**: IT implements technical preservation measures
6. **Documentation**: Document litigation hold procedures

## Record Retention

Legal review records are attorney-client privileged and retained indefinitely:

- **Privileged Materials**: Indefinite retention in secure, privileged repository
- **Published Content**: Retained for life of publication + 10 years
- **Rejected Content**: Retained for 7 years minimum
- **Litigation Hold**: Indefinite retention if litigation pending or threatened
