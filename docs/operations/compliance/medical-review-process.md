# Medical Review Process

## Overview

All educational content in the Growth Training app must undergo medical review by a licensed physician (MD or DO) to ensure medical accuracy, safety, and appropriateness. This document outlines the medical review process, reviewer qualifications, and documentation requirements.

## Purpose

The medical review process serves to:
- Verify medical and scientific accuracy of content
- Ensure safety warnings are adequate and appropriate
- Confirm citations properly support medical claims
- Identify potential health risks or contraindications
- Prevent false or misleading medical claims
- Protect user safety and company liability

## Medical Reviewer Qualifications

### Required Qualifications

All medical reviewers must meet the following minimum requirements:

1. **Active Medical License**: Current, unrestricted medical license (MD or DO) in good standing
2. **Board Certification**: Board certification in relevant specialty preferred (e.g., Urology, Men's Health, Sports Medicine)
3. **Clinical Experience**: Minimum 5 years of clinical practice experience
4. **No Conflicts of Interest**: No financial or personal conflicts that could bias review
5. **Professional Liability Insurance**: Current malpractice insurance coverage

### Preferred Qualifications

- Specialization in urology, andrology, or men's health
- Academic appointment or research experience
- Experience with evidence-based medicine and systematic reviews
- Familiarity with FDA/FTC regulations for health claims

### Reviewer Vetting Process

Before engaging a medical reviewer:

1. **Credential Verification**
   - Verify medical license with state medical board
   - Confirm board certification status
   - Check for disciplinary actions or malpractice history
   - Verify professional liability insurance

2. **Conflict of Interest Disclosure**
   - Complete conflict of interest questionnaire
   - Disclose financial relationships with competitors
   - Disclose personal relationships with company principals
   - Sign conflict of interest agreement

3. **Engagement Agreement**
   - Execute professional services agreement
   - Define scope of review responsibilities
   - Establish compensation structure
   - Clarify liability and indemnification

## Medical Review Checklist

For each article under review, the medical reviewer must evaluate:

### 1. Medical Accuracy

- [ ] **Anatomical Information**: All anatomical descriptions are medically accurate
- [ ] **Physiological Processes**: Biological mechanisms are correctly explained
- [ ] **Scientific Claims**: All medical/scientific claims are supported by evidence
- [ ] **Citation Support**: Citations adequately support the claims made
- [ ] **Current Standards**: Content reflects current medical understanding (not outdated)
- [ ] **Technical Terminology**: Medical terms are used correctly and defined appropriately

**Notes**: _Reviewer documents any inaccuracies found and recommendations for correction_

### 2. Safety Considerations

- [ ] **Risk Disclosure**: Potential risks and side effects are clearly disclosed
- [ ] **Contraindications**: Medical contraindications are identified and explained
- [ ] **Warning Adequacy**: Safety warnings are sufficient for the content
- [ ] **Emergency Guidance**: Appropriate guidance for seeking emergency care
- [ ] **Age Appropriateness**: Content appropriately restricted to adults 18+
- [ ] **Vulnerable Populations**: Special warnings for high-risk populations addressed

**Notes**: _Reviewer identifies any safety concerns or missing warnings_

### 3. Evidence Quality

- [ ] **Citation Quality**: Sources are peer-reviewed, academic, or authoritative
- [ ] **Evidence Level**: Claims match the strength of supporting evidence
- [ ] **Study Limitations**: Limitations of cited research are acknowledged
- [ ] **Conflicting Evidence**: Alternative viewpoints or conflicting data addressed
- [ ] **Human Studies**: Preference for human studies over animal/in-vitro when available
- [ ] **Sample Size**: Studies cited have adequate sample sizes for their claims

**Notes**: _Reviewer assesses overall evidence quality and identifies weak citations_

### 4. Regulatory Compliance

- [ ] **FDA Compliance**: No prohibited disease claims or drug claims
- [ ] **FTC Compliance**: No false or misleading advertising claims
- [ ] **Health Claims**: All health claims are substantiated and appropriate
- [ ] **Guarantee Avoidance**: No guarantees of specific health outcomes
- [ ] **Testimonial Restrictions**: No unsubstantiated user testimonials presented as typical results

**Notes**: _Reviewer identifies any regulatory compliance concerns_

### 5. Liability Assessment

- [ ] **Malpractice Risk**: Content does not constitute individual medical advice
- [ ] **Standard of Care**: Recommendations align with accepted medical standards
- [ ] **Harm Potential**: Low likelihood of user harm from following content
- [ ] **Disclaimer Adequacy**: Medical disclaimers are legally sufficient
- [ ] **Referral Language**: Appropriate recommendations to consult healthcare providers

**Notes**: _Reviewer assesses potential liability exposure from content_

## Review Process Workflow

### Step 1: Content Preparation

Before submitting for medical review:
1. Content is complete and professionally edited
2. All citations are properly formatted and accessible
3. Initial internal review completed
4. Compliance checklist initiated

### Step 2: Medical Review Assignment

1. Assign appropriate medical reviewer based on content area
2. Provide reviewer with:
   - Article content
   - Citation list and full-text references
   - Medical review checklist
   - Review report template
   - Compliance guidelines
3. Set review deadline (typically 7-14 business days)

### Step 3: Medical Review Conduct

Reviewer:
1. Reads content thoroughly
2. Verifies citations and checks references
3. Completes medical review checklist
4. Documents findings in review report
5. Provides detailed feedback and recommendations
6. Returns completed review report

### Step 4: Review Response

Upon receiving medical review report:

**If APPROVED**:
1. File review report in `docs/operations/compliance/medical-reviews/[article-id]-medical-review.md`
2. Update compliance checklist
3. Proceed to legal review

**If APPROVED WITH REVISIONS**:
1. Content team implements required revisions
2. Document all changes made
3. Resubmit to reviewer for verification
4. Obtain final approval
5. File review report
6. Proceed to legal review

**If REJECTED**:
1. Content team assesses whether content can be salvaged
2. Major revision or complete rewrite may be required
3. Resubmit as new content for full review
4. If irreparable, content is archived and not published

### Step 5: Documentation

All medical reviews must be documented and filed:
- Review report saved as `[article-id]-medical-review.md`
- Stored in `docs/operations/compliance/medical-reviews/`
- Compliance checklist updated
- Review added to content audit log

## Periodic Re-Review Requirements

Medical content requires periodic re-review to ensure it remains current:

### Re-Review Schedule

- **Annual Review**: All content reviewed annually for currency
- **Triggered Review**: If new research significantly changes understanding
- **Regulatory Review**: If FDA/FTC guidance changes
- **Incident Review**: If user complaints or issues arise

### Re-Review Process

1. Flag content for re-review based on schedule or trigger
2. Assign to original reviewer if available, or new qualified reviewer
3. Conduct abbreviated review focusing on:
   - New research since last review
   - Changes in medical consensus
   - Updated citations needed
   - Modified risk disclosures
4. Document re-review with date and findings
5. Update content if needed
6. Reset review timer

## Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure

All medical reviewers must:
- Sign Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary content
- Not disclose review participation publicly
- Secure all review materials
- Return or destroy materials after review

## Dispute Resolution

If content creators disagree with medical reviewer findings:

1. **Internal Discussion**: Content team and reviewer discuss concerns
2. **Additional Evidence**: Team may provide additional citations or rationale
3. **Second Opinion**: Senior medical reviewer or medical advisory board consulted
4. **Final Decision**: Medical Director or Chief Medical Officer makes binding decision
5. **Documentation**: All disputes and resolutions documented

## Quality Assurance

To ensure review quality:
- Random audit of 10% of reviews annually
- Reviewer performance evaluated based on:
  - Thoroughness of review
  - Quality of feedback
  - Consistency with other reviewers
  - Timeliness of review completion
- Underperforming reviewers provided feedback or replaced
- Annual reviewer recredentialing

## Record Retention

Medical review records retained per company policy:
- Active content: Review records kept for life of content + 7 years
- Archived content: Review records kept for 10 years minimum
- Legal hold: Indefinite retention if litigation pending
