## 1. Quality Assessment Framework

### 1.1 Evaluation Criteria

Each source was evaluated on five dimensions:

#### A. Peer-Review Status (Weight: 30%)
- **Score 5**: Peer-reviewed in high-impact journal
- **Score 4**: Peer-reviewed in recognized journal
- **Score 3**: Peer-reviewed in specialized journal
- **Score 2**: Editorial review by medical professionals
- **Score 1**: No formal peer review

#### B. Institutional Credibility (Weight: 25%)
- **Score 5**: Top-tier medical institution (Mayo, Cleveland Clinic, etc.)
- **Score 4**: Academic medical center or professional organization
- **Score 3**: Recognized healthcare organization
- **Score 2**: Medical credentials verified
- **Score 1**: Limited institutional backing

#### C. Recency (Weight: 20%)
- **Score 5**: Published 2023-2025
- **Score 4**: Published 2020-2022
- **Score 3**: Published 2015-2019
- **Score 2**: Published 2010-2014
- **Score 1**: Published before 2010 (foundational only)

#### D. Accessibility (Weight: 15%)
- **Score 5**: Full open access
- **Score 4**: Free abstract + accessible summary
- **Score 3**: Abstract available
- **Score 2**: Limited access
- **Score 1**: Paywall with no abstract

#### E. Relevance (Weight: 10%)
- **Score 5**: Directly addresses PE topics
- **Score 4**: Highly relevant adjacent field
- **Score 3**: Applicable principles
- **Score 2**: Tangentially relevant
- **Score 1**: Background only

### 1.2 Overall Quality Score Calculation

**Formula**: (Peer-Review × 0.30) + (Credibility × 0.25) + (Recency × 0.20) + (Access × 0.15) + (Relevance × 0.10)

**Quality Tiers**:
- **Excellent** (4.5-5.0): Highest quality, cite prominently
- **High** (3.5-4.4): Strong quality, reliable sources
- **Good** (2.5-3.4): Acceptable quality, use with context
- **Fair** (1.5-2.4): Limited use, supplement only
- **Poor** (<1.5): Do not use

## 11. Quality Assurance Checklist

### 11.1 Pre-Publication Verification

Before using any source in articles, verify:

✅ **Source Credibility**:
- [ ] Peer-review status confirmed
- [ ] Institutional affiliation verified
- [ ] No predatory journal indicators
- [ ] Author credentials checked

✅ **Content Accuracy**:
- [ ] Key findings accurately represented
- [ ] Context preserved (not cherry-picked)
- [ ] Limitations acknowledged
- [ ] Conflicts of interest noted

✅ **Citation Quality**:
- [ ] Full bibliographic information complete
- [ ] DOI or PubMed ID included where available
- [ ] URL verified and accessible
- [ ] APA 7th edition format correct

✅ **Ethical Standards**:
- [ ] No Reddit content included
- [ ] No plagiarism or improper paraphrasing
- [ ] Proper attribution maintained
- [ ] Medical disclaimers included

### 11.2 Red Flags to Avoid

❌ Sources making extraordinary claims without evidence
❌ Sources contradicting medical consensus without rationale
❌ Sources with undisclosed commercial interests
❌ Sources lacking clear authorship or institutional backing
❌ Sources promoting specific products without disclosure

**Result**: Zero red flags identified in current source collection
