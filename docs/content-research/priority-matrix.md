## Priority Ranking Methodology

### Engagement Score Formula
```
Engagement Score = (Avg Score × 0.3 + Avg Comments × 0.7) × Frequency
```

### Weighting Rationale
- **Comments weighted higher (70%)**: Indicates active discussion and user questions
- **Score weighted lower (30%)**: Shows general interest but less actionable
- **Frequency multiplier**: Ensures topics with sustained interest rank higher
