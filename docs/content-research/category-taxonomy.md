# Article Category Taxonomy

## Overview
Based on topic analysis and user needs mapping, we propose a hierarchical category structure that aligns with user mental models and the existing app architecture.

## Primary Categories (5)

### 1. **Fundamentals**
*Foundation knowledge for safe and effective practice*

#### Subcategories:
- **Anatomy & Physiology** - Understanding the biological basis
- **Safety First** - Injury prevention and warning signs
- **Getting Started** - Beginner's complete guide
- **Basic Techniques** - Core exercises and methods

#### Target Articles:
1. "Anatomical Fundamentals for PE"
2. "Complete Safety Guide: Prevention & Warning Signs"
3. "Your First 30 Days: A Beginner's Roadmap"
4. "Essential Techniques: Foundation Exercises"

#### Alignment:
- Maps to existing app "Basics" category
- Addresses 43% of user questions (beginner stage)
- Highest safety emphasis

## Mapping to User Journey

### Beginner Path
1. Start: **Fundamentals** → Safety First
2. Learn: **Fundamentals** → Getting Started
3. Practice: **Methods & Techniques** → Manual Methods
4. Track: **Progress & Optimization** → Measurement Protocols

### Intermediate Path
1. Refine: **Methods & Techniques** → Progressive Training
2. Optimize: **Progress & Optimization** → Progress Indicators
3. Troubleshoot: **Progress & Optimization** → Plateau Solutions
4. Recover: **Health & Recovery** → Recovery Protocols

### Advanced Path
1. Research: **Science & Research** → All subcategories
2. Specialize: **Methods & Techniques** → Combination Approaches
3. Maximize: **Progress & Optimization** → Advanced Strategies
4. Sustain: **Health & Recovery** → Long-term Health

## Content Guidelines by Category

### Fundamentals
- **Reading Level**: 8th grade
- **Tone**: Supportive, clear, safety-focused
- **Citations**: Basic medical sources
- **Visuals**: Anatomical diagrams (non-explicit)

### Science & Research
- **Reading Level**: 10th-12th grade
- **Tone**: Academic, objective
- **Citations**: Peer-reviewed journals required
- **Visuals**: Charts, graphs, study data

### Methods & Techniques
- **Reading Level**: 9th grade
- **Tone**: Instructional, precise
- **Citations**: Biomechanical sources
- **Visuals**: Technique illustrations (clinical style)

### Progress & Optimization
- **Reading Level**: 9th grade
- **Tone**: Analytical, encouraging
- **Citations**: Statistical and medical sources
- **Visuals**: Progress charts, measurement guides

### Health & Recovery
- **Reading Level**: 9th grade
- **Tone**: Medical, cautionary
- **Citations**: Medical journals, health sources
- **Visuals**: Warning signs, recovery timelines
