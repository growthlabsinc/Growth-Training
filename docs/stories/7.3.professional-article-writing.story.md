### Article Requirements

**Length**: 1500-3000 words per article

**Reading Level**: 8th-10th grade (accessible but scientifically accurate)
- Use clear, direct language
- Define medical terms when first used
- Break complex concepts into digestible sections
- Use analogies and examples where helpful

**Citation Requirements**:
- **Minimum 5-10 citations per article** from Story 7.2 source database
- Use in-text citations: (Author, Year) or (Institution, Year)
- Include full reference list at end of each article in APA 7th edition format
- Prioritize peer-reviewed sources when available
- Use authoritative medical institutions (Mayo, Cleveland Clinic) for patient-focused information

**Structure Requirements**:
Each article must include:

1. **Title** - Clear, engaging, descriptive
2. **Introduction** (200-300 words)
   - Hook to engage readers
   - Overview of topic importance
   - What readers will learn
3. **Main Content Sections** (1000-2000 words)
   - 3-5 major sections with clear headings
   - Each section 300-500 words
   - In-text citations throughout
   - Evidence-based claims
4. **Key Takeaways** (100-200 words)
   - Bullet-point summary of main findings
   - Practical applications
5. **Medical Disclaimer** (Required)
   - Medical consultation advisory
   - No guarantee of results
   - Individual variation acknowledgment
   - Risk disclosure
   - Age restriction (18+)
6. **References**
   - Full APA 7th edition citations
   - All sources from Story 7.2 database
   - Include DOI or PubMed ID when available

**Tone and Style**:
- Professional but accessible
- Evidence-based and factual
- Non-promotional (educational focus)
- Safety-conscious
- Respectful of user intelligence
- Medical accuracy paramount

### Medical Disclaimer Template

Include this disclaimer at the end of EVERY article:

```markdown
---

## Medical Disclaimer

**This article is for educational and informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice.** The information presented is based on scientific research and medical literature but should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

**Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider** with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or before beginning any new health regimen. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read in this article.

**Individual results may vary.** The effectiveness of techniques or protocols discussed in this article can differ significantly from person to person based on genetics, health status, age, adherence, and other factors.

**Potential risks exist.** Any physical intervention carries potential risks. Be aware of warning signs of injury and discontinue immediately if you experience pain, discomfort, or other concerning symptoms.

**Age restriction:** This content is intended for adults 18 years of age and older only.

If you experience any adverse effects or have concerns about your health, seek immediate medical attention.
```

### Source Citation Guidelines

**Accessing Sources**:
- Load `docs/content-research/source-database.json` to access all 62 sources
- Reference `docs/content-research/academic-sources.md` for formatted APA citations
- Select most relevant sources for each article topic

**In-Text Citation Format** (APA 7th edition):
- Single author: (Smith, 2023)
- Multiple authors: (Smith et al., 2023)
- Institution: (Mayo Clinic, 2024)
- Direct quote: (Smith, 2023, p. 45)
- Multiple citations: (Smith, 2023; Jones, 2024)

**Reference List Format** (APA 7th edition):
```
Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article.
   Journal Name, Volume(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xx.xxxx/xxxxx

Institution. (Year). Title of article. Source Name. https://www.url.com
```

**DOI and PubMed ID Inclusion**:
- When source has DOI: Include as `https://doi.org/[DOI]`
- When source has PubMed ID: Note as `PMID: [number]`
- When neither available: Use full URL
