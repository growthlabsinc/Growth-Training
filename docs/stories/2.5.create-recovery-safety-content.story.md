### Recovery & Safety Content Requirements
All recovery and safety methods MUST include:
- Medical consultation recommendation prominent in every exercise
- Age verification requirement (18+)
- Risk acknowledgment language
- Clear contraindications for each technique
- Progressive difficulty levels (start conservative)
- Stop signs for immediate cessation (pain, numbness, discoloration)
- Recovery timing recommendations (24-72 hours for intensive sessions)
- First-aid guidance for common issues (edema, bruising, soreness)
- Integration with existing exercise categories (how recovery applies to length/girth/eq work)
[Source: Epic 2 requirements and safety framework]

### Content Categories Needed
1. **Warm-up Protocols**
   - Heat application methods (warm cloth, warm shower, heating pad)
   - Light stretching routines (preparatory stretches)
   - Blood flow preparation exercises
   - Pre-exercise safety checklists

2. **Cool-down Procedures**
   - Recovery techniques (gentle massage, cold application)
   - Healing optimization (proper rest positioning)
   - Post-exercise care protocols
   - Recovery assessment guidelines

3. **Injury Prevention**
   - Warning signs to watch for during exercises
   - When to stop immediately protocols
   - Progressive intensity guidelines
   - Equipment safety checks

4. **Recovery Integration**
   - Rest day protocols
   - Recovery timing for different exercise types
   - Progressive return from breaks
   - Long-term recovery planning

NOTE: These are individual EXERCISES that will be stored in growth_exercises collection with category="recovery". They are NOT routines. Routines (stored separately) will reference these exercises by their IDs when building structured training programs.
[Source: Epic 2 content structure]
