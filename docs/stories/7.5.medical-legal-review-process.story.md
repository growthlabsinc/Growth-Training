### Required Disclaimers (from Epic 7.5)

Epic 7.5 specifies these standard disclaimers must be present in all educational content:

1. **Medical Consultation Advisory**: "This information is for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Consult with a healthcare provider before beginning any exercise program."

2. **No Guarantee of Results**: "Individual results may vary. This app does not guarantee specific outcomes or results from following the information provided."

3. **Individual Variation Acknowledgment**: "Every individual's physiology is different. What works for one person may not work for another."

4. **Risk Disclosure**: "There are inherent risks associated with physical exercise programs. Stop immediately if you experience pain, discomfort, or unusual symptoms, and seek medical attention."

5. **Age Restrictions (18+)**: "This app and its content are intended for adults 18 years of age and older only."

### Medical Review Process Requirements

[Source: docs/prd/epic-7-learning-center-content-v2.md#story-7-5]

Epic requires a structured 5-step review process:

1. **Medical professional review (MD or DO)**: Licensed physician must review all content for medical accuracy
2. **Legal compliance check**: Legal counsel must verify content complies with regulations
3. **FDA/FTC compliance verification**: Ensure no false health claims or prohibited marketing
4. **Disclaimer adequacy assessment**: Verify all required disclaimers are present and legally sufficient
5. **Risk assessment completion**: Document potential liability risks and mitigation strategies
