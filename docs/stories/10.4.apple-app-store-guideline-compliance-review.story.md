# Story 10.4: Apple App Store Guideline Compliance Review

## Status
Approved

## Story
**As a** Development Team,
**I want** to ensure the app fully complies with Apple App Store Review Guidelines,
**so that** we can successfully submit Growth Training to the App Store without rejections and maintain ongoing compliance.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. A compliance review against App Store Guidelines has been completed and documented
2. Any identified potential issues are addressed before submission
3. App Store submission metadata, including privacy labels, is prepared and accurate

## Tasks / Subtasks

- [ ] Task 1: Conduct comprehensive App Store Guidelines review (AC: 1)
  - [ ] Review Section 1 - Safety (1.4 Physical Harm, 1.5 Developer Identity)
  - [ ] Review Section 5 - Privacy (5.1 Data Collection and Storage, 5.6 Health and Medical)
  - [ ] Review AI-specific guidelines (transparency, content filtering, limitations)
  - [ ] Review Section 2 - Performance (2.1 App Completeness, 2.3 Accurate Metadata)
  - [ ] Document compliance status for each guideline in `docs/app-store-compliance.md`
  - [ ] Verify existing compliance doc is up-to-date with current app version

- [ ] Task 2: Update and verify app-store-compliance.md document (AC: 1)
  - [ ] Update review date and app version in document header
  - [ ] Verify Physical Harm (1.4) compliance: disclaimers in onboarding, method details, timer features
  - [ ] Verify Developer Identity (1.5): contact info in settings, support email accessible
  - [ ] Verify Privacy (5.1): privacy policy in onboarding, consent flow, data minimization
  - [ ] Verify Health and Medical (5.6): no medical claims, appropriate disclaimers, safety warnings
  - [ ] Verify AI guidelines: AI labeling in AICoachDisclaimerView, limitation disclosure, content filtering
  - [ ] Document any new compliance risks or recommendations

- [ ] Task 3: Address identified compliance issues (AC: 2)
  - [ ] Review Medium Priority recommendations from app-store-compliance.md
  - [ ] Implement enhanced safety disclaimers if needed (timer features, first-time users)
  - [ ] Verify privacy policy accessibility in Settings menu
  - [ ] Address any High Priority issues (if identified)
  - [ ] Document resolution of all issues in compliance doc

- [ ] Task 4: Prepare and verify App Store metadata (AC: 3)
  - [ ] Review and update `docs/app-store-metadata.md`
  - [ ] Verify app description accurately reflects current features (no placeholder content)
  - [ ] Verify keywords are relevant and accurate (max 100 characters)
  - [ ] Confirm age rating (17+ for mature health content) and justification
  - [ ] Verify app category (Health & Fitness primary, Lifestyle secondary)
  - [ ] Verify subscription pricing and terms are accurate

- [ ] Task 5: Prepare and verify Privacy Labels for App Store Connect (AC: 3)
  - [ ] Document data types collected:
    - Account Information: Email (linked to user, not for tracking)
    - Usage Data: App Interactions (analytics, anonymized)
    - Health & Fitness: Session logs, measurements (linked to user, not for tracking)
  - [ ] Document third-party data sharing (Firebase/Google for backend, analytics, crash reporting)
  - [ ] Verify all privacy labels match actual data collection practices
  - [ ] Ensure privacy labels section in metadata doc is complete and accurate

- [ ] Task 6: Verify screenshot requirements and guidelines (AC: 3)
  - [ ] Review screenshot requirements from `docs/app-store-screenshot-requirements.md`
  - [ ] Verify existing screenshots represent actual app functionality
  - [ ] Ensure screenshots don't include placeholder content or "coming soon" sections
  - [ ] Confirm screenshots match age rating (17+) appropriateness
  - [ ] Document screenshot status in metadata doc

- [ ] Task 7: Complete pre-submission checklist (AC: 1, 2)
  - [ ] Technical Requirements: App builds for distribution, iOS 16+, signing/provisioning
  - [ ] Content Requirements: Text compliance, appropriate images, no placeholder content
  - [ ] Legal Requirements: Privacy policy accessible, Terms of Service, age rating, COPPA compliance
  - [ ] Metadata Requirements: Description complete, keywords accurate, screenshots ready, privacy labels match
  - [ ] Mark all incomplete items and create follow-up tasks if needed

- [ ] Task 8: Document ongoing compliance strategy (AC: 1)
  - [ ] Define review schedule (monthly features, quarterly full audit, pre-update reviews)
  - [ ] Document maintenance process for compliance documents
  - [ ] Establish process for monitoring App Store Guidelines changes
  - [ ] Create process for updating privacy labels when data practices change

### Compliance Review Guidelines
[Source: docs/operations/compliance/compliance-process.md]

**Compliance Workflow Components:**
1. Medical Review: Licensed physician verification (medical accuracy, safety warnings)
2. Legal Review: Licensed attorney verification (FDA/FTC compliance, liability)
3. Disclaimer Verification: All 5 required disclaimers present
4. Risk Assessment: Document and mitigate liability risks
5. Compliance Checklist: Consolidate all verifications

**Risk Rating System:**
- Critical: Immediate threat to health/safety
- High: Significant injury potential
- Medium: Moderate risk with mitigations
- Low: Minimal risk

   - Confirm age rating matches content (17+ for adult health content)

   - Confirm privacy labels match documented data collection
   - Verify no undocumented data collection occurs
   - Confirm third-party SDKs (Firebase) are accurately represented
