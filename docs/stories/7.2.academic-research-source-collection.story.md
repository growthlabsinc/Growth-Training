### Research Databases to Use

**Primary Medical Databases**:
- **PubMed/MEDLINE** - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
  - Primary source for medical and biomedical research
  - Free access to abstracts, many full-text articles
  - PubMed IDs for permanent reference

- **Google Scholar** - https://scholar.google.com/
  - Broader academic search across disciplines
  - Often provides PDF access links
  - Citation tracking and related papers

**Academic Journal Databases**:
- **JSTOR** - https://www.jstor.org/
  - Academic journal archives
  - Access may require institutional login

- **ScienceDirect** - https://www.sciencedirect.com/
  - Elsevier's platform for scientific journals
  - Strong in medical and life sciences

- **Springer** - https://link.springer.com/
  - Large collection of scientific journals
  - Nature journals included

- **Wiley Online Library** - https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/
  - Medical and scientific journals
  - Some open access content

### Source Quality Criteria

**Required Characteristics**:
- Peer-reviewed publication
- Published in recognized scientific journal
- Accessible DOI (Digital Object Identifier) or PubMed ID
- Author affiliations with reputable institutions
- Methodology section present (for research studies)
- References cited (indicates scholarly rigor)

**Preferred Characteristics**:
- Published within last 10 years (2015-2025)
- Open access or institutional access available
- High journal impact factor (>2.0 preferred)
- Clinical studies or systematic reviews
- Human subjects (where applicable)

**Exclusions**:
- Blog posts, news articles, opinion pieces
- Reddit posts or forum content
- Non-peer-reviewed publications
- Predatory journals (check Beall's List)
- Studies with significant conflicts of interest

**For Safety & Injury Prevention**:
- Search terms: "penile injury", "tissue damage prevention", "safe tissue expansion"
- Target journals: Journal of Urology, BJU International, Sexual Medicine

- Target journals: Tissue Engineering, Journal of Applied Physiology
- Look for basic science research on tissue growth mechanisms

**For Vascular Health**:
- Search terms: "penile blood flow", "erectile function", "vascular health"
- Target journals: Journal of Sexual Medicine, Urology
- Look for cardiovascular research, blood flow studies

**For Biomechanics**:
- Search terms: "mechanical stress tissue", "tissue biomechanics", "traction forces"
- Target journals: Journal of Biomechanics, Clinical Biomechanics
- Look for mechanical loading studies, force application research

**For Recovery Science**:
- Search terms: "tissue recovery", "rest periods exercise", "deconditioning"
- Target journals: Sports Medicine, Journal of Applied Physiology
- Look for recovery protocols, adaptation research

### Citation Database Structure

**Required Fields for Each Source**:
- **Bibliographic Information**:
  - Authors (full names)
  - Year of publication
  - Article title
  - Journal name
  - Volume and issue number
  - Page numbers
  - DOI or PubMed ID

- **Access Information**:
  - URL to DOI resolver (https://doi.org/[DOI])
  - PubMed URL (if applicable)
  - Open access status (yes/no)
  - PDF availability

- **Categorization**:
  - Primary topic category (from Story 7.1 taxonomy)
  - Secondary topic categories (if applicable)
  - Research type (clinical trial, review, case study, etc.)
  - Relevance score (1-5 scale)

- **Quality Metrics**:
  - Journal impact factor (if available)
  - Peer-review status (confirmed)
  - Institution reputation
  - Citation count (from Google Scholar)

**Suggested Format**: JSON or structured Markdown table for easy parsing
